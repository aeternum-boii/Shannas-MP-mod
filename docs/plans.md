# Planned Future Updates

Planned features are scored on a priority from 0 to 10. Priorities 9 and 10 are reserved for critical errors in previous updates that warrant a Hotfix. 

In general this project uses the MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH versioning system. The stable branch and the beta branch on steam may only receive patches during a running saturday campaign. The Alpha branch usually receives nightly builds of the currently developed major or minor version.  

## Major Updates

These are updates of a size, that warrant a new high level version. Usually only one of these updates should ever be published in the same version.

- **Government Reform Rework** - *Priority 8*

  Touching up the entire reform system and increasing interconnectivity of systems related to it. This means in detail, that more government reforms should rely on different means on unlocking them (e.g. idea group combinations, other reforms beforehand).

  Due to the sheer size of this idea, it warrants a major patch.

- **Map Overhauls** - *Priority 4*

  Touching up existing adjacencies, adding new ones and potentially adding / removing provinces. Also adding Jan Mayen as a wasteland to store easter egg monuments in. In general the idea being: Driving more abilities to fight over minor water borders (like more competitive mp mods), reducing detail in areas where no players play and increasing detail in relevant areas.

  Specific ideas: More Canals (Thailand, Russia, Great Lakes?), More Adjacencies in general, Guinea no wasteland, Great Lakes (Africa) wasteland, lake in novgorod, maybe some crossable lakes (there literally is a 'lake' adjacency)

- **Great Project Rework** - *Priority 4*

  Touching up existing Great Projects, making them more interconnected with other systems as well as adding more minor great projects (cheaper but far less impactful).
