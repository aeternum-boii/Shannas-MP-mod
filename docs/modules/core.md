### Ruler Generation

Ruler stats are generated from two `0-3` random rolls, capped at 4, followed by a base `+1`. Each stat then has a 6.25% chance to gain an additional `+1`. Final stats are capped at 6.

| | Normal | Republican Virtues |
|---|---:|---:|
| Expected stat | 3.8125 | 4.8125 |
| Most likely stat | 5 (36.72%) | 6 (39.06%) |
| Chance of >= 4 | 63.67% | 81.64% |
| Chance of >= 5 | 39.06% | 63.67% |
| Chance of 6 | 2.34% | 39.06% |
| Expected total ADM/DIP/MIL | 11.4375 | 14.4375 |
